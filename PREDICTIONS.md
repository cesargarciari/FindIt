# Predictions before running evaluation

- Text-based queries: Expected to perform best, particularly queries containing exact text, because the content is clearly defined and easier to recognize.

- Visual queries: Expected to perform worst because identifying relevant screenshots based on visual descriptions is more challenging, especially when determining the most effective prompts and what it needs to detect what you are looking for.

- Language comparison (English vs. Spanish): Expect Spanish queries to perform worse than English queries because OCR and language models are often more optimized for English content.

- Spanish visual queries: Expect to perform worse than English visual queries due to potential limitations in Spanish-language visual descriptions and semantic understanding.

- Multi-answer queries: Expected the system to retrieve at least one correct screenshot in most cases, even when multiple valid answers existed.

- Retrieval ranking: Expected successful queries to place the correct screenshot within the top five results consistently
